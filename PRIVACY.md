# Privacy

Last updated: October 4, 2026.

**Pan Out doesn't collect data from users' computers: no personal data, telemetry, analytics, or usage reports.** It doesn't send cooking files or conversations to its maintainer or any Pan Out service.

Cook profiles, preferences, household details, recipes, session notes, and lessons are stored in local workspace files. Claude reads these files, and may read cooking conversation logs and photos you provide; Pan Out's maintainer does not. Personal identifiers are not required.

Claude's AI provider processes content read into your session under its own privacy policy and account settings. Research searches may send queries to search providers. Local storage does not mean local AI processing.

You can inspect, edit, or delete workspace files. Uninstalling Pan Out doesn't delete them. Manage conversation history, backups, and synced copies separately.
